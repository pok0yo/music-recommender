# Music Recommender

## Platform
Web application, designed for keyboard, pointer, and touch. English interface; track metadata preserves Unicode.

## Product truth
Choose one recording, explore up to ten connections from shared listening, inspect the method, and use another result as the seed. No account, playback, social features, or feedback collection. The backend's current serving catalog has 215 recordings and 32 approved supported starters. Further reseeding may omit metadata and return a limited list. External listening links are currently unavailable.

## Brand commitments
Y2K Futurism / Soft Club, match feel rather than reference content. Readable controls, icy blue/white surfaces, cobalt typography, sculpted glass enclosures and selective chrome edges. The approved generated design board is a visual reference only. Original comparison routes keep flat CSS reservations. At the user's explicit request, the new Disc Tray artwork demos use four locally stored Higgsfield-generated images; no reference screenshot is copied into the UI. The artwork is decorative and disappears during working states. The working name is Music Recommender.
